PRIVACY INFORMATION

Your information.
Handled with clarity.

This notice explains how information is handled when you use the Baanward website. Updated 17 September 2026.

Who to contact

Baanward Co.LTD. is responsible for handling inquiries submitted to Baanward. For questions about your information or to request access, correction or deletion, email bw@baanward.com.

Inquiry delivery through Web3Forms

When you press Send inquiry, the form sends the details you enter to Web3Forms for processing and email delivery to the inbox configured for Baanward. The form does not save entries in browser storage or a Baanward website database. Your browser may offer autofill under its own settings.

Site access and technical information

The website itself does not add advertising, analytics, tracking cookies or third-party font requests. The hosting service may process request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to deliver and protect the site. A hosted private preview may also use the platform’s sign-in and access controls, governed by that platform’s privacy terms.

Information in your inquiry

The inquiry asks for your name, email, preferred communication method, property location and type, current stage, concern and optional relevant date or listing link. Do not submit deeds, identity documents, financial records or confidential legal files through the initial inquiry.

Who can access your inquiry

Up to three authorised Baanward team members may access your inquiry to respond to you and coordinate the support you request. The service providers described in this notice also process information to deliver the form, email or messaging services.

How long we keep inquiries

Our default is to retain inquiry correspondence, including email and WhatsApp messages, unless you request deletion. This is subject to applicable data-protection requirements, including requirements to delete information when it is no longer necessary or to retain particular records where required by law.

To request deletion, email bw@baanward.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting. If any records must be retained, we will explain the reason in our response.

Service-provider processing and retention

Web3Forms processes form submissions and technical information for delivery and spam prevention. Its policy describes international processing and retention of submission data for up to three years, unless a shorter plan period or earlier deletion applies. See the Web3Forms privacy policy. Copies held by Baanward are covered by our inquiry-retention policy above; service providers apply their own policies to the copies they process.

Email and WhatsApp

You can also contact us by email or WhatsApp. The WhatsApp links open the service only when you choose to use them; this website does not embed a WhatsApp chat widget. Messages sent through these channels are also processed by the relevant email or messaging service.

Specialist referrals

A Baanward inquiry is not a legal engagement. Permission must be obtained before information is shared for a referral. Our Thai legal counsel or legal partner carries out their own conflict checks and engagement process before receiving a substantive legal file.
