ABOUT BAANWARD

Your property in Thailand.
A local partner by your side.

Baanward supports international buyers and owners through independent property checks, purchase coordination and ongoing care — bringing local support and specialist expertise together around you.

ONE RELATIONSHIP, FROM BUYING TO OWNING

A familiar point of contact.
In an unfamiliar process.

A property in Thailand can be an exciting next chapter. Understanding the information, coordinating the people involved and keeping track from overseas can take more time than expected.

01

From first question to the keys

We listen to your concerns, organise checks and visits, coordinate specialists and follow up the next steps. When the purchase is complete, we can remain your local point of contact for the property.

02

Alongside the people already involved

You can work with us on a home you found through an agent, a developer or your own search. We work alongside the people already involved, with your permission and a clear understanding of our role.

INDEPENDENT SUPPORT

Your priorities.
Our starting point.

Baanward is independent of sellers, developers and selling agents. We receive no seller-side commissions. Our work begins with your questions and the support you choose.

We keep information sources visible, bring questions to the right people and agree costs and authority before acting. You remain in control of decisions and approvals.

THE RIGHT PEOPLE FOR THE RIGHT QUESTIONS

Local coordination.
Professional expertise.

01

What Baanward handles

Baanward handles property visits, information gathering, developer and project research, communication and practical follow-up. We coordinate trusted legal specialists when professional legal review is required, and technical specialists for the assessments your property needs.

02

What specialists handle

Our separately engaged Thai legal counsel or legal partner handles legal due diligence, title and contract review, legal opinions and ownership-structure advice. Technical specialists carry out the inspections and assessments agreed with them.

Baanward is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We ask permission before a legal referral; specialist acceptance, conflict checks and scope are confirmed separately, with specialist costs included in your quote. The lawyer we assign accepts the engagement before receiving a substantive legal file. We do not offer property listings or sourcing.
