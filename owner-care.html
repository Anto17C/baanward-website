BUILT AROUND YOUR SITUATION

Work with the people
already involved.

You do not need to live overseas to ask for help. Local owners can use the same support for a single task, a busy period or a regular care schedule.

What to keep in mind

A condition visit cannot detect everything or monitor changes between visits. Emergency attendance, technical assessments and repair costs must be expressly agreed. Full rental and tenant management are not part of the launch service.

CLEAR BEFORE WE START

Your scope. Your quote.

The quote depends on location, property type, the concern, urgency, number of visits and specialist input. We agree the coordination period, reports, access and approval limits before starting.

Legal work, technical inspections, repairs and materials are separately scoped unless explicitly included. No published rates or automatic subscriptions apply.
