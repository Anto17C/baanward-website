PROPERTY CHECKS & PURCHASE SUPPORT

Before you commit,
get a clearer picture.

Understand the property you have found and the questions worth asking. We bring together independent checks, local research and specialist reviews, then help coordinate your next steps.

01

BEFORE YOU BUY

Property Checks & Research

An authorised site visit or live video viewing helps you see the property in context. We focus on your priorities, record what is visible and follow up the information you need.

  • Your plans and concerns discussed before the visit.
  • Dated photographs of visible condition, access and surroundings.
  • Available information from the seller, developer or building management, with sources identified.
  • A summary of observations, unanswered questions and recommended specialist checks.

02

DURING PURCHASE

Purchase & Translation Support

We help organise practical purchase tasks, with communication and translation support to make information easier to follow. You remain in control of decisions and approvals; any negotiation or representation needs a specifically agreed mandate.

  • Seller, agent and developer communication within your instructions.
  • Appointment, inspection and document coordination.
  • Translation support for property communication and documents; legal interpretation stays with your legal adviser.
  • A task list showing responsibilities, outstanding information and arrangements towards handover.

DEVELOPER & PROJECT RESEARCH

Understand the information
behind the project.

We gather available background information about the developer or project, review the information supplied and follow up claims or gaps that matter to your decision. Your summary identifies sources, dates and questions still to resolve.

Legal status, authority to sell and ownership questions are referred to your appointed lawyer for professional verification. Research is not a guarantee of a developer’s future performance.

PROPERTY DUE DILIGENCE COORDINATION

The right checks.
Before your next decision.

Questions about the seller, ownership, contract or condition deserve the right expertise. We organise the review process and keep outstanding questions in view.

Baanward coordinates the process. Appointed specialists provide their own findings and advice under separate engagements. A document received is not yet a fact verified.

  • Agree the questions and review scope; arrange specialist introductions with your permission.
  • Coordinate document requests, property access and review appointments.
  • Follow up missing information and open questions with the appointed specialists.
  • Keep you informed of review status, findings received and the next agreed steps.

YOUR PROPERTY. YOUR EXISTING TEAM.

Keep your agent.
Add the support you need.

We support the home you have already chosen and can work alongside your agent, developer, lawyer and building management. A standard visit records visible observations; legal title, structural condition and investment value require the appropriate professional assessment.

CLEAR FROM THE START

Your plans.
Your scope and quote.

We agree the tasks, visits, reports, timing and quote before work begins. Location, urgency and the support you need determine the scope. Specialist fees, repairs, materials and other third-party costs are identified separately unless expressly included. Additional work and spending require approval.
