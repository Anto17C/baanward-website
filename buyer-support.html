BUYING OFF-PLAN OR NEW-BUILD

Know what you are relying on before you pay.

When much of the price is paid before title is transferred, the contract and the evidence behind each payment matter. We help you gather that evidence and prepare the right questions for our lawyer, who advises on the contract. We cannot guarantee that a developer will perform or repay.

Questions to settle before each payment

  • What objective evidence will confirm each construction or approval milestone before an instalment falls due?
  • Whether any escrow, bank guarantee or other security protects payments made before transfer.
  • Which approvals are already in place and which are still outstanding.
  • What happens to your payments if approvals, completion or foreign-ownership registration cannot be achieved.
  • How the completion date can be extended, and for what reasons.

Our lawyer advises on the contract wording, your remedies and which amendments are worth requesting. A refund right is only as useful as the seller’s ability to meet it, so we also help you gather the available information about the project, the seller and its approvals.

How we help

  • Obtain the documents and milestone evidence the developer agrees to provide, and record what has and has not been supplied.
  • Where agreed, our technical specialists confirm construction progress against the payment schedule.
  • Relay the amendment requests you approve and track the developer’s replies, within an agreed mandate.
  • Return the revised contract to our lawyer to check that the agreed protections were included before you sign.
  • Keep your payment instructions and transfer records organised.

BEFORE YOU TAKE OVER

Handover without surprises.

Completion and handover can move quickly, sometimes while you are abroad. We help you prepare so that defects and costs are recorded before ownership is registered.

At inspection

  • Check whether the contract treats a missed inspection period as acceptance, and ask our lawyer how your defect and warranty rights are protected.
  • Arrange an authorised representative or independent inspector to attend if you cannot.
  • Prepare a written snagging list for the developer to acknowledge, with a timetable for repairs.

Before ownership is registered

  • Ask for an itemised list of transfer fees and taxes, and who pays each.
  • Budget for the sinking fund and common fees on top of the purchase price, based on the final registered area.
  • Confirm the order of final payment, delivery and registration with our lawyer.
  • Keep records of every international transfer showing it is for your unit.

A snagging list and inspection record are practical records, not a structural survey or legal opinion. Technical assessments come from our technical specialists where included.

One quote for your agreed service.

Are legal and technical specialist fees included?+

Your total quote includes their fees for the agreed work. Our lawyer’s separate legal engagement defines their responsibilities to you; it does not mean an additional fee for that same work. We confirm any additional work and costs for your approval before proceeding.

If my contract promises a refund, do I still need due diligence?+

A contract remedy and verification do different jobs. A refund promise is only as strong as the seller’s ability to pay it, and a title or approvals check shows the position at the time it is made, so checks can be repeated before key payments or transfer where you want that. Our lawyer advises on your contract rights. We cannot guarantee any outcome.

Who follows up amendments with the developer?+

Our lawyer advises which amendments are worth requesting. With your approval, we relay your requests, track the developer’s replies and return the revised contract to our lawyer to check. Any negotiation or representation needs a specifically agreed mandate.

Can someone inspect my condo at handover if I cannot attend?+

Yes, where agreed and available. We can arrange an authorised representative or independent specialist, subject to the developer’s access and any contract requirements. Our lawyer advises how inspection deadlines affect your rights. A handover visit is not a structural survey unless our technical specialist’s assessment is agreed.
