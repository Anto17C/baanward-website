House & Condo Purchase Checks & Due Diligence Services in Nonthaburi

Bring the home, its management arrangements and the proposed purchase into one organised review. We coordinate independent visits and specialist input for a house or condo you have found in Nonthaburi. Our lawyer and technical specialists carry out the agreed reviews, with Baanward managing the service throughout.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Understand the home and its shared arrangements.

For a condo, the questions may extend beyond the unit to building management. For a house, they may include land documents, estate facilities and access. We help identify which information is needed, who can supply it and which specialist should assess it.

01

Agree the viewing priorities

We arrange an authorised visit around your intended use of the home.

  • Record visible condition and practical access
  • Note questions about shared areas available to view
  • Separate viewing observations from technical inspection
02

Follow the information to its source

We coordinate requests to the seller, agent and any relevant estate or building manager.

  • Request available rules, fees and maintenance information
  • Gather property documents for lawyer-led review
  • Record information that has not been supplied
03

Prepare the next purchase step

We organise specialist appointments and practical follow-up before the agreed decision point.

  • Coordinate technical inspection where required
  • Track responses to our lawyer’s document requests
  • Prepare handover and access questions for follow-up

DUE DILIGENCE SCOPE

Legal review, technical checks and clear follow-up.

We agree the review around the property, proposed transaction and documents available. Your quote includes Baanward’s fee and all specialist fees for the agreed scope, including our lawyer’s agreed work where legal review is included. Any additional work or spending requires your approval.

Legal due diligence by our lawyer

Our lawyer performs the agreed legal review, with the fee included in your total quote. The legal engagement defines their professional responsibilities without adding a fee for the same work. Depending on the property and scope, questions may include:

  • Land or unit title, seller authority and recorded encumbrances
  • Proposed ownership or lease arrangements and purchase contract
  • Available building or estate documents, with access and shared-property questions as applicable

Our lawyer determines the required legal checks, explains the findings and advises on signing or payment questions.

Inspections by our technical specialists

Where included, our specialist assesses condition within a defined inspection brief. We coordinate the practical arrangements:

  • Match the inspection brief to the house or condo you are buying
  • Specify the building areas and installations to be assessed
  • Record technical findings, access limitations and follow-up questions

A viewing does not replace a technical inspection. Our technical specialist confirms the assessment methods, access requirements and limitations.

Your service, findings and next steps

We collect available documents, organise access and appointments, and follow up seller or management responses. You receive the agreed review record: documents received and missing, findings attributed to our lawyer or technical specialist, unresolved questions and practical next steps. Baanward does not issue legal opinions or technical certifications.

PLAN THE REVIEW

Keep the property review and your plans connected.

Tell us whether the Nonthaburi home will be occupied immediately or left empty for a period after purchase. That helps shape practical questions about keys, access, utilities and the point at which ongoing care might begin.

For a managed estate or condominium, give us the management contact if you have it. We request the agreed information and distinguish the seller’s statements from documents or responses supplied by management.

If you are also considering a Bangkok condo, each property remains a separate assignment within the agreed scope. The Bangkok condo page explains that unit-and-building review process in more detail.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Know what is answered and what is still open.

Your record brings together visit observations, the source of available management information, specialist findings and unresolved items. Our lawyer assesses legal rights, ownership arrangements and the purchase documents.

You have one main point of contact at Baanward for the agreed work. Our lawyer provides legal findings and advice, and our technical specialists provide their assessments. Their agreed fees are included in your total quote. Our lawyer’s separate legal engagement defines their responsibilities to you; it does not mean an additional fee for the same agreed work. A practical visit does not replace a technical inspection.

BUYING A HOUSE OR LAND?

Line up the title and the building together.

Check the title deed, the house registration book and the building permit together, and ask whether the building matches the approved plans. Our lawyer advises on title and ownership structure; our technical specialists assess the building where included.

PREPARE YOUR ENQUIRY

Start with the property you have found.

  • House address, estate or condo name in Nonthaburi
  • Listing and proposed purchase arrangements
  • Seller, agent and management contacts if available
  • Your timing and plans for occupation or time away

A short description is enough to begin. We confirm the documents needed and how to share them after discussing your scope.

SCOPE AND COSTS

Agree the work before it begins.

Your total quote includes Baanward’s fee and all legal and technical specialist fees for the agreed work. We confirm access, visits, specialist availability, reports, timing and any travel costs. Extra work or spending outside that scope requires your approval.

Can you check shared fees and estate rules?+

We can request available information from the relevant contacts and record their responses. Legal interpretation belongs to our lawyer; we do not guarantee that every requested document will be supplied.

Can the visit include a technical inspection?+

Yes, when our specialist and inspection scope are agreed. An ordinary viewing is not a substitute for that inspection.

Can you arrange care after purchase?+

Yes. Condition visits and maintenance coordination can be scoped separately once ownership, access and your instructions are confirmed.
