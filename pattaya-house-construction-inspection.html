House Construction Inspection & Oversight in Pattaya

Already building a house in Pattaya? We help arrange an independent inspection of the work completed so far, bring the findings together and coordinate the next steps with your builder.

Start Your Consultation ↗

SUPPORT AROUND YOUR NEXT STEP

A useful checkpoint, even midway through the build.

You do not need to have involved us from the beginning. Tell us the current construction stage and what concerns you. We agree the access, available plans and specialist input needed to understand the work that can be inspected. Support can begin with one inspection appointment or continue through agreed progress visits.

01

Start with the work so far

We gather your questions, available drawings, builder updates and photographs, then coordinate an inspection with an appropriately engaged specialist.

  • Current build stage and areas of concern
  • Plans and specifications available for review
  • Access agreed with the owner and builder
02

Turn findings into follow-up

We organise the specialist findings and practical questions into an open-items record, so you can see what needs a response and who is following it up.

  • Dated photographs and supporting documents
  • Builder responses kept alongside each item
  • Reinspection arranged where agreed
03

Keep later stages in view

If you want ongoing support, we agree a visit schedule around the next stages of your house build or renovation.

  • Visible progress recorded at agreed visits
  • Contractor appointments and access coordinated
  • Next update date and outstanding decisions

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A record you can act on.

You receive the agreed visit updates, the appointed specialist’s report where included, and a clear record of outstanding items and follow-up responsibilities. We distinguish our observations, statements from the builder and specialist conclusions.

Baanward coordinates visits and practical follow-up. Technical inspection findings, engineering supervision and certification belong to the appointed specialist. A visit cannot establish the condition of concealed work or certify a payment milestone unless the specialist’s agreed assessment covers it.

SCOPE, COSTS AND QUESTIONS

Clear arrangements before work begins.

Your quote is one total price including Baanward’s fee and the specialist costs involved in the agreed work. We confirm visits, reports, timing and any travel costs. Repairs, materials and other costs outside the scope are quoted separately; additional work requires your approval.

Can you help if construction has already started?+

Yes. We start from the current stage and available records. The specialist identifies what can be assessed and any limits caused by concealed or inaccessible work.

Can I arrange a one-off inspection?+

Yes. A single inspection coordination scope can be quoted, with follow-up visits or reinspection added only when agreed.

Do you replace my builder or provide structural certification?+

No. We work alongside your existing builder. Any technical judgment or certification must come from an appropriately engaged specialist.
