PROPERTY SUPPORT, FROM BUYING TO OWNING

Buy with confidence.
Own with peace of mind.

Property checks, due diligence services, purchase support, oversight and care in Thailand. One main point of contact manages your agreed work, including our lawyer and technical specialists, under one total quote.

Before You Buy

Property checks & verification support

Build a clearer picture before committing. Buyer Support brings together property visits, developer and project research, available information and our lawyer and technical specialists.

During Purchase

Purchase coordination

Keep communication, appointments and documents moving between you and the people involved. Buyer Support includes translation assistance, the agreed specialist work and follow-up through the practical purchase steps.

After Purchase

Property care & local representation

Property Oversight helps with progress, handover and contractor supervision. Property Care provides scheduled condition visits, maintenance coordination and local representation within your agreed mandate. Emergency support is available until 11pm Thailand time.
