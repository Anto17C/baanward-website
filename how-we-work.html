BEFORE A VISIT OR TASK

Agree the details
that matter.

  • Property and purpose: location, stage, concerns and the questions to answer.
  • Permission and access: who can authorise visits, hold keys and communicate on your behalf.
  • Scope and timing: visit frequency, coordination period, reports and next update.
  • Costs and approvals: our quote, separately scoped specialists and spending limits.
  • Boundaries: what a practical check can establish, what it cannot and how unresolved items are handled.

PROPERTY VERIFICATION, STEP BY STEP

Know what is checked.
Know what is still open.

Found a property you want to buy? Start with a clearer picture of the property, the seller or developer, and the information behind their claims.

Baanward coordinates: the agreed property visits, document collection and follow-up, with information sources and unanswered questions clearly recorded.

Your lawyer verifies: once you become a confirmed client and the separate legal engagement is accepted, our Thai legal counsel or legal partner undertakes the agreed legal due diligence.

Each update shows: documents received, checks in progress, findings and the next action. Receiving a document does not mean its contents have been verified.

Legal findings come from the appointed lawyer. Technical assessments, where needed, come from an appropriately scoped specialist.

THE RIGHT HELP, CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES

Practical care.
Specialist expertise when needed.

Baanward provides property verification coordination, buyer support and ongoing owner care in Thailand. Baanward is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Legal due diligence and other legal services are carried out by separately engaged Thai legal counsel or legal partners, subject to conflict checks and acceptance of the matter.

Technical inspections and certification need an appropriately scoped specialist. We help coordinate that input and keep responsibilities clear.

We ask your permission before a legal referral. The appointed legal adviser completes their acceptance process before receiving a substantive legal file. Specialist fees are separately scoped unless expressly included.
