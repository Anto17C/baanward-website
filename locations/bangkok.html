NATIONWIDE COVERAGE

A dense, transit-linked
condominium market.

Bangkok’s residential market is dominated by condominiums along the BTS and MRT lines, from new-build towers to older resale buildings, plus a smaller stock of townhouses. Many buyers purchase under the foreign freehold quota, and a large proportion of owners—whether investors or relocating professionals—are based overseas or in another Thai province for long stretches of the year.

Baanward coordinates property verification, buyer support and ongoing owner care in Bangkok the same way as our original coverage areas: an authorised visit, a practical summary of what was seen, and a clear next step—arranged around your availability and confirmed in your scope and quote.

WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU START

Local considerations
in Bangkok.

  • The foreign freehold quota in a building can be full—this is worth confirming before you commit to a unit.
  • Older resale buildings and new developments carry different practical considerations for building condition and management.
  • Sinking funds, common-area fees and juristic-person records are worth reviewing before a purchase decision.
  • Access and visit timing in central Bangkok buildings usually requires advance coordination with the management office.
  • Legal due diligence and technical inspection are separately scoped specialist work, coordinated on your behalf when needed.
