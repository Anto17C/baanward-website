A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we begin.

Do I need to buy through Baanward?+

No. We support a property you have already found or own. We do not offer property listings or sourcing.

Can you work alongside my existing agent?+

Yes. We can coordinate with your appointed agent, seller, developer or building management within an agreed scope. Any negotiation or representation needs your agreed mandate.

Where can you help?+

Our initial focus is Pattaya, Chonburi and Rayong. We confirm location eligibility, access and availability for each inquiry. Other locations are on request.

Is a visit a legal or engineering check?+

No. A practical visit records visible observations. Legal advice and due diligence are handled separately by Walailak Law Firm, subject to conflict checks and engagement. Technical assessments require an appropriately scoped specialist.

Can I ask for one-off help?+

Yes. You can request a single property visit or a bounded coordination task, as well as scheduled oversight or ongoing owner care.

What will my report include?+

The agreed report can include dated photographs, observations, information supplied by others, unanswered questions and an issue tracker with next steps. Specialist findings are identified separately.

How are fees agreed?+

We quote for your property, location, concern, urgency, visit frequency and coordination period. Legal work, technical inspections, repairs and materials are separately scoped unless explicitly included. No work starts until the scope and quote are agreed.
