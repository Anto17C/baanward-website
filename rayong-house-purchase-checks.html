SUPPORT AROUND YOUR NEXT STEP

Understand the property before moving ahead.

A house purchase can involve several people, different sets of documents and a limited window to visit Thailand. Whether you have shortlisted a home in Rayong or already agreed the next purchase steps, we organise the questions, visits and specialist input around your situation. We can work with your existing agent and seller.

01

Check the home you have chosen

We arrange an authorised property visit, collect available practical information and record questions that need an answer before you proceed.

  • Visible condition and access observations
  • Available information on utilities and common areas
  • Specialist inspection coordination where needed
02

Coordinate the document review

We gather available property documents and organise review by the appointed lawyer and technical specialists within the agreed scope.

  • Title and ownership questions routed to the lawyer
  • Building documents requested for specialist review
  • Missing information and seller responses tracked
03

Prepare the purchase appointments

We help organise the practical steps and communications around the transaction, including buyers travelling for a short stay.

  • Document and appointment checklists
  • Translation support and communication follow-up
  • Coordination with the lawyer, seller and agent

DUE DILIGENCE SCOPE

Legal review, technical checks and clear follow-up.

We agree the review around the property, proposed transaction and documents available. Your quote is one total price including Baanward’s fee and the specialist costs for the agreed work. Extra work requires your approval.

Legal due diligence by the lawyer we assign

The lawyer performs the agreed legal review under a separate legal engagement. Depending on the property and scope, questions may include:

  • Land title, seller authority and recorded encumbrances
  • Proposed ownership or lease arrangements, access rights and purchase contract
  • Available building documents and any estate arrangements relevant to the transaction

The lawyer determines the required legal checks, explains the findings and advises on signing or payment questions.

Technical checks by the appointed specialist

Where included, the appropriate specialist assesses condition within a defined inspection brief. We coordinate the practical arrangements:

  • Agree the building inspection brief and authorised access
  • Specify any pool, utilities or other installations to be assessed
  • Record technical findings and any areas the specialist could not inspect

A viewing does not replace a technical inspection. The specialist confirms the assessment methods, access requirements and limitations.

Baanward’s coordination and your review record

We collect available documents, organise access and appointments, and follow up seller or management responses. You receive the agreed review record: documents received and missing, findings attributed to the lawyer or technical specialist, unresolved questions and practical next steps. Baanward does not issue legal opinions or technical certifications.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A record you can act on.

Your agreed scope brings together visit observations, available documents, specialist findings and unresolved questions. You receive a practical next-step checklist, with legal conclusions clearly attributed to the lawyer and technical conclusions to the relevant specialist.

The appointed lawyer verifies title, ownership, encumbrances and any proposed lease or other registered rights. Baanward does not determine which legal arrangement is suitable or guarantee ownership protection. Technical specialists assess building matters within their accepted scope.

SCOPE, COSTS AND QUESTIONS

Clear arrangements before work begins.

Your quote is one total price including Baanward’s fee and the specialist costs involved in the agreed work. We confirm visits, reports, timing and any travel costs. Repairs, materials and other costs outside the scope are quoted separately; additional work requires your approval.

Can you help before I pay a deposit?+

Yes. Tell us about any proposed deadline so we can confirm what visits and specialist reviews can be arranged before you decide. Advice about signing or paying comes from your appointed lawyer.

Can you help if family members are involved in the purchase?+

Yes. We coordinate information and appointments for the agreed participants. The lawyer advises on their respective rights, legal arrangements and any conflicts.

Do I need to buy through Baanward?+

No. We support a property you have found yourself or through another agent. We are independent of sellers and receive no seller-side commissions.
